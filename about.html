John Ese Efekoha is a Nigerian forward who can play both as a striker and an attacking midfielder. He is comfortable using either foot, has a strong physical presence, and is effective in aerial challenges.

His game is built around good stamina, composure on the ball, and the ability to create opportunities in attacking areas. His versatility allows him to contribute in different roles across the forward line.
